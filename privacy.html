FocusLock

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

The short version: FocusLock has no server and no account database. Everything the app learns about you stays on your device.

Information we do not collect

FocusLock does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no third-party tracking in the app. We cannot see your app usage, your health data or your earned minutes.

Screen Time and app blocking

FocusLock uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls and Managed Settings) to block the apps you choose. Selecting apps happens in Apple's own picker; Apple returns only opaque tokens, so the app — and we — never learn which apps you selected. Your selection is stored in the app's local container on your device.

Health data

With your permission, FocusLock reads step count, workouts and mindful minutes from Apple Health to convert activity into screen time, and writes completed mindfulness sessions back to Health. Health data is processed on your device only. It is never uploaded, shared or used for advertising.

Sign in with Apple

FocusLock uses Sign in with Apple so you can start your trial. Apple may share your name and an email address (or a private relay address) with the app. This information is stored locally on your device and is not sent to any server operated by us.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit. FocusLock never sees your payment details. Apple provides the app with a signed receipt so it can verify that your subscription is active. Apple's own privacy policy governs the data Apple processes for payments.

Children

FocusLock is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

You can revoke Screen Time or Health access at any time in the iOS Settings app. Deleting FocusLock removes the data it stored on your device, including your earned minutes and the apps you selected.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
