FocusLock turns the time you spend focusing into time you can spend on your phone.

How does FocusLock work?

Pick the apps that eat your day — social, video, games. They stay blocked until you earn minutes back by walking, exercising, studying, staying off your phone, or completing a short mindfulness session.

Why is an app still blocked after I earned time?

Open FocusLock once after earning time. FocusLock releases the shield through Apple Screen Time, and iOS applies the change immediately. If nothing changes, make sure Screen Time access is still granted: Settings → Screen Time → FocusLock.

Is my app usage sent anywhere?

No. App blocking is handled on your device with Apple's Screen Time framework. Which apps you select is stored locally, and Apple deliberately hides the names of apps even from FocusLock.

How do I restore my subscription?

Open the paywall in FocusLock and tap “Restore Purchases”. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel my subscription?

FocusLock Pro is $2.99 per month and renews automatically. Cancel any time in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your iPhone. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the current period stays active until it ends.

How do I reset my data?

Delete the app to remove everything FocusLock stored on this device, including your earned minutes and the apps you selected.
